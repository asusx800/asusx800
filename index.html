What I'm building

Infrastructure Sentinel Systems (ISS)

A Malaysian InfraTech startup building an agentic Document-AI and 3D Digital Twin platform for predictive structural monitoring of highway infrastructure.

  • Document-AI turns inspection and maintenance records into structured, verified data.
  • Verification and traceability: every extracted record can be reviewed, confirmed or corrected by an engineer.
  • 3D Digital Twin links asset data to a model of the structure for predictive monitoring.
  • Cloud-native architecture designed with data sovereignty in mind.

GroundTruth

A hackathon project that applies document-AI to rank township infrastructure assets (drainage, retaining walls, roads, slopes) by failure risk using historical maintenance records.
